About This Dashboard

This dashboard visualizes the AI Architecture Health Index from the GAIF v1.0 specification. AAHI is a continuously computed composite metric that quantifies the overall governance health of an enterprise AI architecture across six dimensions.

The worked example preloaded here matches Section 6.7 of the GAIF specification. Add your own systems to compute your organization's AAHI.
